/// ---------- Entry 1 -- February-28-2026 ----------

# TOOLS USED:
# 3x MtM cables (2 large - green, 1 small - brown)
# 1x pushbutton
# 1x LED light (green)
# Arduino Uno

I had taken inspiration from the potentiometer Adjustable Blinking Light project, and
wondered what it would be like to create a light circuit with a push button input. My aim
was to test how the pushbutton effects the current; whether or not it stops the flow of
current, or allows it.

I assembled a basic blinking light circuit, but adjusted it for the pushbutton. For this
project, I placed the pushbutton over the ravine of the breadboard. But I would like to
try and see if it would work even if the button weren't crossing the ravine (i.e., the
pushbutton is entirely on one side of the breadboard).
